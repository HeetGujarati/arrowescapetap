Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 v1.0.0

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Zenlio Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you play Arrow Escape.

We respect your privacy. Arrow Escape is designed to function with minimal data collection. We do not sell your personal data. By downloading and playing Arrow Escape, you agree to the practices described in this document.

Data Collection

Arrow Escape itself does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to our servers.

  • Game Progress: Levels, scores, and daily streaks are stored strictly on your local device.
  • Preferences: Haptic and audio settings are stored locally.

If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress will be permanently deleted unless backed up via native OS mechanisms (e.g., Google Play Games cloud save).

Analytics

To improve game stability and understand player engagement, we utilize third-party telemetry services. These services collect anonymized data.

  • Usage Data: In-game events, screen views, and session lengths.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, performance metrics, and device specifications (OS version, device model).

App Permissions

Arrow Escape requests only the minimum permissions required to function:

  • INTERNET: Necessary for fetching advertisements and submitting anonymous crash reports.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by ad SDKs to verify network connectivity before requesting ad payloads.

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or internal file storage.

Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape is a general audience game and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our advertising partners are configured to serve non-personalized, family-safe advertisements.
